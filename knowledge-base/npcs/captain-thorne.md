Captain Thorne is the gate-warden of Stormwall. She has held the position for eleven years, the longest unbroken tenure in the town's recorded history. Before that she served as a caravan guard on the southern route, where she lost the use of her left hand to frostbite during the storm that earned her the captaincy.

Thorne's authority is informal but absolute. She decides who is allowed through the north road in winter, which in practice means she decides who lives and who dies during the gale season. She is brusque, fair, and entirely without sentiment. She has turned away her own cousins when the weather demanded it.

She keeps an office in the watchtower of the upper district. Petitioners are required to state their business in under thirty seconds, and Thorne expects them to know what they are asking for before they walk in. She has been known to throw imprecise petitioners physically down the tower stairs. They are usually not seriously injured.

Thorne does not speak to outsiders about the Crystal Caverns. When asked, she answers that the matter is not within her jurisdiction. Pressed further, she will direct the questioner to Elder Maren and then end the conversation. Locals interpret this as a warning. Outsiders rarely take it that way.
