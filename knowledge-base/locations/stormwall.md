Stormwall is the largest settlement in the Frostmere and the only one with a working deep-water harbour. The town takes its name from the autumn gale that batters the seawall every year, never quite breaching it but never quite leaving either. Roughly four thousand people live here in winter, more in summer when the trade caravans come up from the south.

The town is built on three terraces cut into the cliff. The lower district holds the harbour, the warehouses, and most of the Glasswright workshops. The middle district is the residential quarter, where the long-standing families have their houses. The upper district contains the meeting house, the Sundered Council's shrine, and the watchtower.

There is no garrison. Stormwall has never had a standing army. Order is maintained by the harbour-master's bailiffs in the lower district, by tradition in the middle, and by the council in the upper. When that fails, which it does several times a year, the citizens close their shutters and wait.

The gate-warden of Stormwall is Captain Thorne. She is the closest thing the town has to a single authority, but she would object to the description. Her real job is to decide who is allowed through the north road in winter, when the weather makes the wrong decision lethal.
