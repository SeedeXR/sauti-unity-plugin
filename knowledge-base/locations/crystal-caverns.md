The Crystal Caverns lie north of Stormwall, hidden beneath the frozen lake. Their entrance is reachable only between the second and fourth weeks of midwinter, when the ice thickens enough to bear a traveller's weight but the meltwater shafts have not yet collapsed.

Inside, the caverns open into a series of chambers lined with pale blue crystal growth. The crystals are not ornamental. They hum faintly to the touch and grow back if broken. Anyone who lingers too long among them begins to hear voices that do not seem to come from inside their own head. The voices are not malicious, but they are not friendly either.

The caverns are widely believed to be a Seep site of the first magnitude. Practitioners who have survived a night inside report visions of events that have not yet happened. Most do not survive a night inside. The single most-repeated warning given to outsiders is this: Elder Maren knows the location of the lost artifact, but she will only speak after dark.

The Sundered Council has tried to seal the caverns three times. Each attempt has failed within a season. The current consensus is that the caverns refuse to be sealed, and the council has stopped trying.
