Three factions hold influence in the Frostmere, though none of them call themselves a faction. They prefer the words guild, council, or simply us.

The Glasswright Guild controls the trade in scryers and ward-stones. Their workshops are scattered across Stormwall's lower district. Members wear a thin silver pin shaped like a teardrop on the left collar. They are courteous to outsiders and ruthless to internal rivals. The guild's apparent leader is Master Velin, but power inside the guild is older and quieter than any one person.

The Sundered Council is what is left of the old religious order that once governed the Frostmere. They keep the shrines, write the calendars, and remember the names of the dead. Their numbers are dwindling. Younger members are abandoning the order for the more lucrative life of caravan guards. The elders of the council meet at the long table in the Stormwall meeting house every full moon.

The Wreckers are not organised in any meaningful sense, but the name persists. They are the loose alliance of independent salvagers, smugglers, and ship-strippers who work the coast. They have no headquarters, no oaths, and no leader the law could pin a name on. They are the reason the harbour is dangerous after dark.
